### amber-dna-myth — placeholder + mislabel fixed (2026-07-26)

Both figure captions shipped with literal `Credit TK` / `Crédito por confirmar` placeholders in EN and ES. Both are now filled from the build record above.

The second caption also **presented the pictured bee as *Proplebeia dominicana* carrying an orchid pollinarium** — i.e. as the actual Ramírez et al. 2007 specimen discussed in that section. It is not: it is *Oligochlora semirugosa*, a different genus, not carrying a pollinarium. The caption (EN + ES) now names the species actually shown and states plainly that it is not the Ramírez specimen.

**The real specimen was NOT substituted in.** Checked and not available under any free licence: Wikipedia's `Meliorchis` article carries no image, Commons has no photograph of the specimen, and the only published images are Fig. 1 of Ramírez et al. 2007 (© Springer Nature) and an unattributed Harvard University handout photo distributed with the 2007 press coverage. Under the standing "real image even if ©, credited" rule this *could* be self-hosted the way the Buffetaut et al. figure was for `two-headed-reptile` — but that is a licensing call for the owner, and no named photographer is recorded for the Harvard handout, so nothing was invented to fill it. **Open decision, not a silent substitution.**

## the-bone-wars (Day 1, 2026-07-13) — RESOLVED 2026-07-30

This post's four images were **never recorded in this file**. Added 2026-07-26 during the pre-push fix pass, when three of the four were left open. **All four were resolved on 2026-07-30** — see the method note below.

| the-bone-wars | *Diplodocus* mount, Smithsonian, from a 1978 slide (`bonewars-diplodocus.jpg`, 1571×1107) — Commons `File:Diplodocus (10436615093).jpg` | **Gary Todd**, via Wikimedia Commons | **CC0 1.0** — verified at Commons |

**Three findings, all closed.**

1. **Fixed 2026-07-26.** The *Triceratops* caption claimed **"CC BY 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons" with no author named**. That is wrong on both counts. The file is the white-background cutout `LA-Triceratops mount-2.jpg` (4560×3460, ratio 1.3179 — an exact aspect match to the 3840×2914 blog copy), which is **CC BY-SA 3.0** and requires naming Allie_Caulfield and User:MathKnight. Both candidate CC BY 4.0 files by Vanadium Erbium (`A Triceratops skeleton.jpg`, `Mounted Triceratops Skeleton.jpg`) are 4000×1848 and are not cutouts, so neither can be the source. Captions corrected EN + ES.
2. **Closed 2026-07-30 — the CC0 claim was correct all along.** The earlier note said `bonewars-diplodocus.jpg` matched nothing in the build's `credits.json` and that "neither Gary Todd's Smithsonian shots (3888×2592, CC0) nor Dosseman's Berlin shot (5673×3782) matches its 1571×1107 geometry." That comparison used the wrong Gary Todd files. **SHA256 of the blog copy is `03f7454a5826fba3975a84b66768f4be5ca58e352d1ab4c878990f0fa09171ee`, byte-identical to the build's `diplodocus_02.jpg`**, whose `credits.json` entry names Commons `File:Diplodocus (10436615093).jpg`. Commons was then checked directly: **Gary Todd, CC0 1.0, original 1,571 × 1,107** — an exact dimension match, so the blog file is the Commons original, not a crop. The caption now names Gary Todd. *(The museum visitors in the lower right are a video-build framing rule, not a licensing question, and do not affect CC0.)*
3. **Closed 2026-07-30 — the other two.** `bonewars-elasmosaurus.jpg` (sha `2c1f6c5b…`) is byte-identical to the build's `elasmosaurus_01.jpg` = Commons `File:Cope Elasmosaurus.jpg`, **Edward Cope, public domain**; the caption now names him. `bonewars-rivals.jpg` (sha `83eb8139…`) is byte-identical to the build's `rivals.jpg`, which is **not in `credits.json`** — it is a PaleoDex-made composite, and inspection shows it uses *photographic* portraits, not the engravings held as `cope_01.jpg`/`marsh_01.jpg`. Its components therefore remain unidentified. Rather than assert an unverifiable source, **the caption no longer says "via Wikimedia Commons"** and instead states the basis that *is* certain: 19th-century photographic portraits of two men who died in 1897 and 1899 are public domain by age.

**Method note.** Every blog image in this post was matched to its build-set original by **SHA256**, not by eyeballing dimensions — which is what produced the false alarm in finding 2. Licences were then confirmed **at Wikimedia Commons itself**, not from the local `credits.json`, per the standing rule that a local record of a claim is not verification of it.

The blog's `bonewars-*.jpg` files are a separate crop/composite set from the video build's per-image files in `paleodex-content/content-grid/2026-07-13-bone-wars/images/multi/credits.json`, which is why they do not map one-to-one by name — but three of the four do map exactly by hash.
